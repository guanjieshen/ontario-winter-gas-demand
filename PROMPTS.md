# Starter prompts

Staring at a blank chat? Copy one of these into **Genie Code** and go. There's nothing to fill in: Genie Code finds your data, and the workspace skills point it at the right schemas and steps.

Each prompt is a conversation starter. Once Genie Code answers, keep chatting: "go with option 2", "do step 1", "show me".

> [!TIP]
> - **Open Genie Code full screen:** `https://<workspace-host>/editor/folders/workspace?o=<workspace-id>&mode=chat`
> - **Open Genie One with a prompt already typed:** `https://<workspace-host>/one/chat?o=<workspace-id>&q=<url-encoded prompt>`

## Find an idea

```text
Hi, I'm at a hackathon and looking for ideas. Find what data I have access to, and give me 3 to 5 hackathon ideas I could build in a couple of hours. For each one, tell me the business problem, the data it uses and what the demo would show.
```

```text
Ask me which team I work on and what slows us down. Then look at the data I can access and suggest hackathon projects that would make my team's day-to-day work easier.
```

```text
Which of the data I have access to is the most interesting for a hackathon, and why? Show me something surprising in it.
```

## Design my project

```text
I have a hackathon project in mind. Ask me to describe it, then help me design it: what data I need and whether I have it, which Databricks features to use, and the steps to build it in a few hours.
```

```text
I have a hackathon idea but I'm not sure it's doable in a day. Ask me 3 or 4 questions about it, then tell me what to build, what to skip, and what the simplest version of the demo looks like.
```

```text
What's the difference between a Genie space, an AI/BI dashboard, AI functions, a Knowledge Assistant and a Databricks App? Which ones should I use for my hackathon project, and why?
```

## Understand the data

```text
Give me a tour of the data for my hackathon project. For each table, explain in plain English what it holds, how the tables connect, and anything I should watch out for.
```

```text
Check the quality of the data I'm using: missing values, duplicates, odd values, and gaps in dates. Tell me what could trip me up.
```

## Start building

```text
I'm new to Databricks. Help me take the first step on my hackathon project. Explain what you're about to do before each step, and keep it simple.
```

```text
Build me a clean table for my project that combines the data I need, with a description on every column. Show me a few sample rows so I can check it.
```

```text
I have free-text data (notes, comments or documents). Show me how AI functions can classify, summarise or pull information out of it, and try it on a small sample first.
```

```text
Help me predict or forecast something useful with my data. Suggest what's worth predicting, build a simple first version, and tell me how accurate it is.
```

## Share it

```text
Help me set up a Genie space so my team can ask questions about my project's data in plain English. Include instructions, example questions and the answers I should expect.
```

```text
Build me an AI/BI dashboard that tells the story of my project: a few headline numbers, a trend, and a detail view.
```

## Get unstuck

```text
I'm stuck. Look at the error in my notebook, or ask me what I'm trying to do and what's happening. Explain what's wrong in plain English and fix it.
```

```text
These numbers don't look right. Check my work for mistakes like double-counting or missing rows, and walk me through it.
```

## Get ready to demo

```text
Look at what I've built today and write me a 3-minute demo script: the problem, what we found, what we built, and why it matters. Suggest what to show on screen at each step.
```

```text
Pretend you're a hackathon judge. Ask me the 5 hardest questions about my project, then help me answer them honestly.
```
